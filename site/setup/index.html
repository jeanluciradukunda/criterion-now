A setup guide for humans

Criterion, from South Africa, in five steps.

The Criterion Channel only opens its doors to the US and Canada. So we send your browser on a small holiday to Canada, buy a ticket, and you watch Kurosawa from the couch. No technical skills needed, only about 20 minutes, a bank card and a computer with Chrome (phones can't add Chrome extensions, so do the setup on a laptop or desktop).

01

Get a VPN

A VPN makes websites think you are somewhere else. We want "somewhere else" to be Canada.

Any of these three will do the job: each has servers in Canada, an official Chrome extension and a 30-day money-back window. The person who wrote this guide uses Proton VPN, which is why the later steps show it, but the steps are the same shape for all three.

  2. Make an account with an email address and a password you will remember. Write the password down somewhere safe (a password manager, or the back of a Kurosawa DVD).

You should now have: a VPN account, and an email saying welcome.

02

Make a Chrome profile just for films

Think of it as a separate little cinema inside Chrome.

Why bother? A Chrome profile is a separate Chrome with its own extensions, bookmarks and logins. The VPN extension only works inside the profile it is installed in, so only your Criterion window takes the trip to Canada. Your banking, email and everything else stay at home in South Africa, exactly as they are now.

  1. Open Chrome. In the top right corner, click your profile picture (a circle with a letter or a little person).
  2. Click Add (or Add Chrome profile).
  3. Click Continue without an account. No Google sign-in needed.
  4. Type a name, for example Criterion, pick a colour you like, and click Done.

You should now see: a brand new Chrome window in the colour you picked, with your new profile picture in the top right. Every step from here happens in this window.

Next time: to get back here, click the profile picture in any Chrome window and pick Criterion.

03

Send that window to Canada

Install the VPN's Chrome extension in your new profile and switch it on.

  1. In the new Criterion window, go to chromewebstore.google.com and search for your VPN's name. (The links in the table open in whichever window you click them in, so skip them for this bit.)
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Click the puzzle piece in the top right, then the pin next to your VPN, so its icon stays in view.
  4. Click the VPN icon and sign in with the account from step 1.
  5. Choose Canada from the country list and click Connect.

You should now see: the extension saying it is connected, with Canada as your location, and its icon in the Criterion window only, not in your usual one. Congratulations, your browser has emigrated. It did not even need a visa.

04

Buy your ticket

Still in the Criterion window, with the VPN connected.

  1. Go to criterionchannel.com.
  2. Click Subscribe (or Start free trial if one is offered) and choose monthly or yearly.
  3. Make your Criterion account with an email and a password.
  4. Enter your card details. A South African card has gone through fine with the VPN on.

You should now see: the Criterion home page full of films. If instead it says the channel is "not currently available in your location", the VPN is off or not set to Canada: go back to step 3.

If your card is declined: check the VPN is still connected and try again; if it still fails, your bank may be blocking foreign online payments, which you can usually switch on in your banking app.

The small print: using a VPN to reach a service from outside its regions may go against the Criterion Channel's terms of use.
